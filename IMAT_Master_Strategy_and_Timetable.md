> **Official Scoring Rule:**  
> • **+1.5 points** for each correct answer  
> • **−0.4 points** for each incorrect answer  
> • **0.0 points** for unanswered questions  
>  
> *Mathematical Reality of Guessing:* If you can eliminate **2 incorrect options** out of 5, your expected value is:  
> $$E = \left(\frac{1}{3} \times 1.5\right) - \left(\frac{2}{3} \times 0.4\right) = 0.50 - 0.267 = +0.233 \text{ points (Statistically favorable to guess)}$$  
> If guessing purely blind (1 out of 5):  
> $$E = \left(\frac{1}{5} \times 1.5\right) - \left(\frac{4}{5} \times 0.4\right) = 0.30 - 0.32 = -0.02 \text{ points (Negative expected value - leave blank)}$$

## ⚡ Strategic 4-Tier Syllabus Hierarchy

### 🔴 Tier 1: Non-Negotiable Core (Must Master 100%)
*Accounts for ~65% of test points. High score-per-hour yield.*

1. **Biology - Cell Biology & Division:**
   - Eukaryotic vs Prokaryotic ultrastructure and organelle functions (mitochondria, ribosomes, Golgi, lysosomes, ER).
   - Cell cycle phases (G1, S, G2, M).
   - Chromosome & DNA molecule counting across Mitosis (prophase to cytokinesis) and Meiosis I & II (homologous pairs, sister chromatids, crossing over, independent assortment).
2. **Biology - Genetics & Molecular Biology:**
   - Central Dogma: DNA replication, transcription, translation, genetic code, mRNA/tRNA/rRNA.
   - Mendelian genetics, monohybrid/dihybrid crosses, Punnett squares, sex-linked traits, pedigree charts.
3. **Biology - Human Physiology:**
   - Cardiovascular system (cardiac cycle, systemic vs pulmonary, vessel pressure).
   - Nervous system (action potential, resting potential, neurotransmitters, synapses).
   - Endocrine system (major hormones, feedback loops: insulin/glucagon, thyroid, pituitary).
   - Respiratory & Kidney/Excretory systems (gas exchange, nephron filtration/reabsorption).
4. **Chemistry - Stoichiometry & Quantitative Basics:**
   - Mole calculations, molar mass, Avogadro's constant.
   - Balancing redox and standard equations.
   - Limiting reactants, theoretical yield, molarity/concentration calculations.
5. **Chemistry - Chemical Bonding & Periodic Trends:**
   - Ionic, covalent (polar vs non-polar), metallic, coordinate bonds.
   - Intermolecular forces: London dispersion, dipole-dipole, Hydrogen bonding.
   - Electronegativity, ionization energy, atomic radius trends across periods and groups.
6. **Chemistry - Acids, Bases & Solutions:**
   - pH and pOH calculations ($-\log[H^+]$, $-\log[OH^-]$).
   - Strong vs weak acids/bases, buffer mechanism concepts, neutralization stoichiometry.
7. **Chemistry - Organic Chemistry Fundamentals:**
   - Functional groups identification (alcohols, aldehydes, ketones, carboxylic acids, esters, amines).
   - IUPAC nomenclature of alkanes, alkenes, alkynes, structural isomerism.

---

### 🟠 Tier 2: High-Yield Problem Solvers (Very Important)
*Accounts for ~20% of test points. Predictable question structures.*

1. **Physics - Mechanics & Energy:**
   - Kinematics in 1D ($v = u + at$, $s = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^2$, $v^2 = u^2 + 2as$).
   - Newton's Laws ($F = ma$, action-reaction, friction).
   - Work, Kinetic & Potential Energy ($W = Fd$, $KE = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$, $PE = mgh$), Conservation of Mechanical Energy.
2. **Physics - Electricity & DC Circuits:**
   - Coulomb's law, electric potential ($V = IR$).
   - Resistors in series ($R_{eq} = R_1 + R_2$) vs parallel ($\frac{1}{R_{eq}} = \frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{R_2}$).
   - Electrical power ($P = IV = I^2R = \frac{V^2}{R}$).
3. **Physics - Fluids:**
   - Density, hydrostatic pressure ($P = \rho gh$), Pascal's principle, Archimedes' buoyant force ($F_b = \rho V g$).
4. **Mathematics - Core Algebra & Functions:**
   - Quadratic equations ($x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}$), factoring, simultaneous linear equations.
   - Powers, exponent rules, logarithms ($\log(ab) = \log a + \log b$).
   - Linear ($y = mx + c$) and quadratic graphs, slopes, intercepts.
5. **Mathematics - Arithmetic, Proportions & Geometry:**
   - Ratios, percentages, scientific notation shortcuts.
   - Pythagorean theorem, triangle similarity, area/volume of circles, cylinders, spheres.

---

### 🟡 Tier 3: Moderate Yield / Quick Formula Coverage
*Accounts for ~10% of test points.*

1. **Physics - Waves & Optics:**
   - Wave formula ($v = f\lambda$), sound waves, reflection, refraction (Snell's Law $n_1 \sin \theta_1 = n_2 \sin \theta_2$), thin lens focal length.
2. **Physics - Thermodynamics:**
   - Temperature scales ($K = ^\circ C + 273$), heat transfer ($Q = mc\Delta T$), ideal gas law ($PV = nRT$).
3. **Chemistry - Equilibrium & Kinetics:**
   - Dynamic equilibrium, equilibrium constant ($K_{eq}$), Le Chatelier's principle (shift with pressure, temperature, concentration).
4. **Chemistry - Electrochemistry:**
   - Oxidation numbers, galvanic cell principles, cathode (reduction) vs anode (oxidation).
5. **Biology - Evolution & Ecology:**
   - Natural selection, genetic drift, speciation, trophic pyramids, food webs, nitrogen/carbon cycles.
6. **Mathematics - Probability & Statistics:**
   - Independent vs dependent probability, mean, median, standard deviation concepts.
7. **Mathematics - Basic Trigonometry:**
   - $\sin, \cos, \tan$ values for $0^\circ, 30^\circ, 45^\circ, 60^\circ, 90^\circ$, basic right-angled triangle problem solving.

---

### 🟢 Tier 4: Low Preparation Return (Practice-Only)
*Accounts for ~5% of test points.*

1. **Logical Reasoning (5 Qs):**
   - Syllogisms, conditional logic ("if P then Q"), sequence patterns, assumption identification.
   - *Strategy:* Practice 20–30 minutes every 2–3 days using past BMAT/IMAT papers. Do not study thick theoretical textbooks.
2. **Reading & General Knowledge (4 Qs):**
   - Reading comprehension passages: finding main author argument, evaluating evidence.
   - General knowledge: Nobel prizes, fundamental historic milestones, key international organizations (UN, WHO, EU).
   - *Strategy:* Read 1 scientific article (Nature, BBC Health, Economist) per day; do not memorize encyclopedias.

## 🔁 Active Recall & Spaced Repetition (The 1-3-7-21 Cadence)

Never just read notes passively. Implement the **1-3-7-21 Review Rule**:
1. **Day 1 (Initial Study):** Learn theory, create bulleted flashcards/summaries, solve 15 immediate practice problems.
2. **Day 3 (First Recall):** Cover notes; write down key diagrams/formulas on a blank sheet from memory (Blurting method).
3. **Day 7 (Application Check):** Solve 10 timed exam questions from past IMAT/BMAT papers without formulas.
4. **Day 21 (Long-Term Retention):** Quick flashcard run + 5 challenging multi-step problems.

## 💡 Quick Tactical Hacks for High-Frequency IMAT Questions

### 🧬 Biology: The Chromosome & Chromatid Counting Matrix
For a human cell ($2n = 46$):
* **G1 Phase (Interphase):** 46 Chromosomes | 46 Chromatids | 46 DNA molecules (Diploid $2n$)
* **S & G2 Phase:** 46 Chromosomes | 92 Chromatids | 92 DNA molecules (Replicated diploid $2n$)
* **Mitosis Metaphase:** 46 Chromosomes | 92 Chromatids | 92 DNA molecules (Aligned at equator)
* **Mitosis Anaphase:** **92 Chromosomes** | 92 Chromatids (temporarily separated individual chromosomes!) | 92 DNA molecules
* **End of Mitosis (Each Daughter Cell):** 46 Chromosomes | 46 Chromatids | 46 DNA molecules ($2n$)
* **Meiosis Metaphase I:** 23 Bivalents / 46 Chromosomes | 92 Chromatids
* **Meiosis After Telophase I (Secondary Spermatocyte/Oocyte):** 23 Chromosomes | 46 Chromatids (Haploid $n$)
* **Meiosis After Telophase II (Gamete):** 23 Chromosomes | 23 Chromatids | 23 DNA molecules (Haploid $n$)

### 🧪 Chemistry: Fast Stoichiometry Shortcuts
* No calculator allowed on IMAT! Master rounding and powers of 10:
  * Avogadro's constant: $6.02 \times 10^{23} \approx 6 \times 10^{23}$
  * Gas molar volume at standard conditions: $22.4 \text{ L/mol}$ or $24.0 \text{ L/mol}$ at RTP.
  * $pH = -\log[H^+]$. If $[H^+] = 2.0 \times 10^{-4}$, $pH = 4 - \log(2) \approx 4 - 0.3 = 3.7$.
* Strong Acids to memorize: $HCl, HBr, HI, HNO_3, H_2SO_4, HClO_4$. All others are weak.
* Strong Bases to memorize: Group 1 Hydroxides ($LiOH, NaOH, KOH$) and $Ba(OH)_2, Ca(OH)_2$.

### ⚛️ Physics & Math: Essential Mental Formula Bank
* Velocity-displacement without time: $v^2 = u^2 + 2as$ (Assume $g \approx 9.8 \text{ or } 10 \text{ m/s}^2$).
* Mechanical Work & Power: $W = Fd \cos \theta$, $P = \frac{W}{t} = Fv$.
* Circuits: Remember **V = IR** and **P = IV = I²R = V²/R**. Identical parallel resistors $R$: equivalent is $R/N$.
* Geometry shortcuts: In a $30^\circ-60^\circ-90^\circ$ triangle, sides are $1 : \sqrt{3} : 2$. In a $45^\circ-45^\circ-90^\circ$ triangle, sides are $1 : 1 : \sqrt{2}$.
