============================================================

ZERO SETUP REQUIRED:
- No database creation in cPanel needed.
- No editing of config.php needed.
- No passwords needed.

HOW TO RUN:
1. Upload "public_html_files.zip" into your hosting "public_html" folder.
2. Right-click the zip and click "Extract" (or copy the files from this folder).
