Why 2026 is Rated 8.6 Effort Index

Mathematical impact of negative marking at a 70.1 cutoff

In the 2011–2022 Cambridge era, candidates gained admission with scores between 42.5 and 52.8 points (~55% net accuracy). Candidates could afford to skip or guess on entire disciplines.

With the 2026 cutoff reaching 70.1 points (Milan/Bologna 74+), candidate accuracy must exceed 82% net (+1.5 correct, −0.4 incorrect).

  • Requires at least 48 to 51 correct answers out of 60.
  • Maximum allowable errors across the entire exam: 3 to 4.
  • Each wrong guess loses 1.9 points against a correct question.
