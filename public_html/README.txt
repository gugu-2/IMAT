IMAT EXAMINATION ROUTINE & SYLLABUS MASTERY ENGINE
===================================================

HOW TO HOST THIS ON YOUR SERVER:
1. Upload all files from this folder directly into your server's "public_html" directory.
2. Create a MySQL Database in your hosting cPanel (MySQL Database Wizard).
3. Open "config.php" and enter your Database Name, Database User, and Password in lines 10-13.
4. Visit your website URL in any browser (phone, tablet, computer):
   https://yourdomain.com/
5. Done! Everything is 100% online, automatically creates its tables, and syncs across all devices!
