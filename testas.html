Understand Your Examination Format First

TestAS is administered in two formats with distinct subtest types, item counts, and timing allocations.

💻 Digital Core Module ~90 Minutes Total

Contains 60 items across three equally timed subtests (20 items each, 25 minutes each):

  • Figure Sequences: Movement, rotation, colour & compound rules
  • Mathematical Equations: Systems of equations, integers 1–20
  • Latin Squares: 5×5 grid deduction, letters A, B, C, D, E
⚙️ Digital Engineering Module 90 Minutes Total

Presents 10–15 testlets, each containing 4–8 related questions based on:

  • Formulas, technical illustrations & circuit diagrams
  • Buoyancy, fluid density & mechanical arrangements
  • Applied reasoning over memorized university formulas
📝 Paper-Based Core Test 110 Minutes (88 Qs)
  • Quantitative Problems: 22 Qs · 45 mins
  • Inferring Relationships: 22 Qs · 10 mins (Strict speed!)
  • Completing Patterns: 22 Qs · 20 mins
  • Continuing Numerical Series: 22 Qs · 25 mins
📐 Paper-Based Engineering 150 Minutes (70 Qs)
  • Formalising Interrelationships: 22 Qs · 60 mins (31.4%)
  • Visualising Solids: 26 Qs · 30 mins (37.1% questions, 20% time)
  • Analysing Interrelationships: 22 Qs · 60 mins (31.4%)
